# Privacy Policy for MyCalc History

**Last updated: 11 December 2025**

MyCalc History (“the App”) is a simple calculator application with a built-in calculation history feature. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data. By using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

MyCalc History **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**.  
Specifically, the App does **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Financial information  
- Location data  
- Contacts or device identifiers  
- Usage analytics  
- Any data from your device or other applications  

---

## 2. Calculation History

The App stores your calculation history **locally on your device only**.

- No history data is uploaded or transmitted to external servers.  
- You can clear the history at any time from within the App.  
- Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App **does not use** any third-party services such as analytics tools, advertising libraries, or cloud storage.  
This means no data is shared with external companies.

---

## 4. Permissions

MyCalc History does **not request any device permissions**.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Since the App does not collect any data, it is safe for use by children.  
We comply with COPPA and similar regulations by not collecting or transmitting personal information of users under 13.

---

## 6. Security

Because all data stays on your device, your information remains entirely under your control.  
We recommend keeping your device secure with a screen lock or other system-level protections.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this GitHub page.  
Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
